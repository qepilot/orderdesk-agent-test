# Correcting an invoice that was issued with a wrong amount

Issued invoices are legal documents.

- Never change `invoices.total_cents` with an UPDATE and never delete an invoice.
- A wrong invoice is corrected by a credit note for the full amount and a new invoice. Finance triggers this
  with the reissue job; engineering supplies the list of order ids and the correct totals.
- First establish what the customer actually paid (captures), because a wrong invoice does not always mean
  a wrong charge. If the charge was right, no money moves; only the document is replaced.
- Fix the code before reissuing, otherwise the new invoices are wrong too.
