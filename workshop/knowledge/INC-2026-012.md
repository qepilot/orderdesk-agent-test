# Cart and receipt differed by one cent

## What happened
About 0.4% of receipts showed a total one cent different from the cart.

## Root cause
Tax was calculated with floating point numbers in the cart and converted to cents by cutting off the decimals,
while the receipt service rounded. Values like 16.9575 became 16.95 in one place and 16.96 in the other.

## Fix
Tax calculation moved to integer cents with one shared helper that rounds half up.

## Lessons
- Never convert money with `int()` or floor division; that truncates. Round half up, explicitly, in one place.
- Test prices with odd cents (x.95, x.99), not only round numbers. Round numbers hide rounding bugs.
- The discount calculation was not migrated in this change and should be reviewed (follow-up was never scheduled).
