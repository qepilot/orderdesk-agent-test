# Customer reports a double charge

Most "charged twice" reports are not double charges.

1. List the payment events of the order. An **authorization** only reserves money. A **capture** takes it.
2. If there is one successful capture, the customer was charged once, whatever their banking app shows.
3. A **voided** authorization (for example after a 3-D Secure retry) can stay visible as a pending entry on the
   card statement for 3 to 5 business days and then disappears. No refund is needed or possible, because no
   money was taken.
4. Only two successful captures for the same order are a real double charge. Escalate to the payments team.

Answer template: "We charged your card once. The second entry is a reservation that your bank will release
within 3 to 5 business days."
